🏓 3S PICK-A-BALL — BOOKING & COURT RULES

Please read before booking. By paying for your slot, you agree to follow all of our rules and policies.

  1. 💳 FIRST PAID, FIRST RESERVED
    Your slot is only considered reserved once payment has been made. No payment means no reservation.
  2. 🚫 ALL BOOKINGS ARE FINAL & NON-REFUNDABLE
    Once your booking is confirmed and paid, it is considered SOLD. Bookers cannot cancel or request a refund.
    If you can no longer use your reserved slot, you may sell or transfer your slot to someone else at your own arrangement. Please inform us of the change.
  3. ⚠️ UNFORESEEN CANCELLATION / INTERRUPTION
    Bookings may only be cancelled or interrupted by 3S Pick-A-Ball due to unforeseen circumstances beyond our control, such as earthquakes, severe weather, natural disasters, or other safety-related situations.
    If an unforeseen situation occurs while you are already playing, the remaining unused time of your paid booking will be rescheduled to another available date and time, subject to our scheduling availability.
  4. ⏰ RESPECT YOUR RESERVED TIME
    Please arrive on time and end your game on time. Your booking starts and ends according to your reserved schedule. Late arrival does not extend your playing time.
  5. 🚭 NO SMOKING & NO ALCOHOL
    Our facility is strictly NO SMOKING and NO ALCOHOL. Please help us keep the court safe, clean, and family-friendly.
  6. 🧹 KEEP THE COURT CLEAN
    Please clean as you go. Dispose of your trash properly and leave the court clean for the next players.
  7. 🔇 KEEP THE NOISE RESPECTFUL
    Have fun and enjoy the game, but please keep your voices and cheering at a respectful level, especially for nearby players and residents.
  8. 🤬 NO FOUL OR OFFENSIVE LANGUAGE
    Please maintain a friendly and respectful environment. No excessive cursing, offensive words, or inappropriate behavior.
  9. 👨‍👩‍👧 CHILDREN MUST BE SUPERVISED
    Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising children at all times while inside the facility.
  10. 🎒 TAKE CARE OF YOUR BELONGINGS
    Please check your belongings before leaving. 3S Pick-A-Ball is not liable for any lost, stolen, or damaged personal items.
  11. 🤝 RESPECT EVERYONE
    Treat fellow players, guests, staff, and other people at the facility with respect and courtesy. No bullying, harassment, or disruptive behavior.
  12. 🏓 TAKE CARE OF THE COURT & EQUIPMENT
    Please use the court and facilities properly. Do not intentionally damage, misuse, or tamper with any court property or equipment. Any damage caused by a player or their group may be charged to the responsible party/booker.

💛 PLAY FAIR. PLAY SAFE. PLAY WITH RESPECT.
