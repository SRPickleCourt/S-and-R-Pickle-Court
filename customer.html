🏓 S and R Pickle Court Rules & Guidelines

Please read and understand the following rules before proceeding with your court reservation.

  1. Reservations are subject to availability and successful payment.
  2. Please arrive on time and observe your reserved schedule.
  3. Respect other players, staff, and nearby residents.
  4. Keep the court and surrounding areas clean.
  5. Take care of court facilities and equipment.
  6. Smoking and disruptive behavior are not allowed on court premises.
  7. Children must be supervised by a responsible adult.
  8. Players are responsible for their personal belongings.
  9. Any cancellation or rescheduling request must be coordinated with court management.
  10. Follow staff instructions and observe safe playing practices.

Agreement is required before proceeding with your reservation.
